Engineered Specification: Adversarial Pre-Mortem & Decision Stress-Testing

Task: Conduct a rigorous, adversarial Pre-Mortem and Trade-Off Analysis for a proposed strategic business decision, suppressing sycophancy and highlighting non-obvious operational fragilities.

Context & Strategic Initiative:
- Proposal: [e.g., Transitioning client onboarding from a high-touch human model to a fully automated self-service SaaS portal]
- Current Operating State: [e.g., 200 high-ACV enterprise accounts, high NPS, constrained customer success capacity]
- Capital & Time Allocation: [e.g., $300k budget, 6-month delivery deadline]
- Core Objective: [e.g., Scale customer intake by 4x without doubling support headcount]

Adversarial Directives:
1. Assume it is 18 months post-launch and the project has catastrophically failed. Unpack the exact chain of failure.
2. Prohibit generic risks (e.g., do not cite "market competition" or "poor communication"). Focus on concrete mechanical bottlenecks, stakeholder misalignment, and cognitive biases.
3. Challenge core management assumptions regarding user adoption speed and self-service willingness.

Output Architecture:
1. The Autopsy: Root-Cause Failure Mechanisms: Detail the 3 most probable sequences of operational breakdown.
2. Assumption Stress-Testing Matrix: Markdown table with columns [Foundational Assumption | Latent Flaw in Assumption | Leading Early-Warning Indicator].
3. Asymmetric Mitigation Plan: Prioritized tactical safeguards to deploy immediately to protect downside risk while maintaining strategic upside.
