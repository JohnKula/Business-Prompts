Engineered Specification: Technical-to-Executive Translation Briefing

Task: Translate complex technical engineering realities or opaque corporate jargon into an objective Executive Impact Briefing for non-technical leadership.

Audience & Communication Goals:
- Audience: [e.g., Chief Executive Officer, VP of Sales, Board Members]
- Technical Domain: [e.g., Database deadlock causing API response latency spikes / Corporate restructuring announcement]
- Critical Concern: [e.g., Will this affect customer SLA renewals or end-of-quarter revenue?]

Translation Guidelines:
1. Eliminate all domain jargon, abstract acronyms, and passive corporate spin without diluting the underlying reality.
2. Convert architectural issues into direct operational dimensions: Cost, Customer Experience, Timeline, and Strategic Risk.
3. Explicitly surface what the source text omits or conceals (e.g., unstated project delays, unhedged operational exposure).
4. Frame the technical bottleneck as a direct trade-off requiring a business decision.

Input Source Text:
"""
[Paste original technical explanation or corporate memo]
"""

Output Architecture:
1. Plain-Language Reality (The Bottom Line): Two-sentence translation explaining exactly what happened and why it matters in commercial terms.
2. Operational Impact Translation: Markdown table with columns [Technical Issue | Translated Business Impact | Financial or Client Exposure].
3. Decisions Required From Leadership: Clear bullet points detailing non-technical decisions or capital approvals necessary to unblock engineering.
