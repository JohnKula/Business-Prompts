Engineered Specification: Evidence-Based Compensation & Role Realignment

Task: Construct an evidence-based business case memorandum for compensation adjustment or role realignment, framed around organizational value delivery and market parity.

Context & Inputs:
- Current Title & Tenure: [Insert Title, e.g., Senior Systems Engineer, 2.5 years]
- Target Title / Compensation Band: [Insert Target Details]
- Primary Manager Persona: [Insert Style, e.g., Analytical, metrics-driven, constrained by division budgets]

Quantifiable Contributions:
- Metric 1: [e.g., Reduced core service latency by 38%, saving $140k annually]
- Metric 2: [e.g., Mentored 4 junior engineers, leading to zero team turnover]
- Metric 3: [e.g., Took on technical ownership of Project Titan following staff departure]

Third-Party Market Benchmark: [e.g., Radford/Levels.fyi 75th percentile for region]

Drafting Parameters:
1. Maintain an objective, collaborative, and analytical tone.
2. Position the adjustment as a mutual strategic investment, not personal entitlement or retention ultimatum.
3. Directly anchor every monetary or level adjustment request to documented business performance.
4. Propose a clear, low-pressure conversation pathway with sufficient lead time for managerial budget review.

Output Architecture:
1. Strategic Meeting Request Email: Max 150 words requesting a focused conversation during performance/budget cycle.
2. One-Page Value Ledger Briefing Attachment: Structured outline summarizing (1) Scope Expansion Over Time, (2) Measurable Value Realized, and (3) Market Reference Alignment.
