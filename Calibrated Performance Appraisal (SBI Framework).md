Engineered Specification: Calibrated Performance Appraisal (SBI Framework)

Task: Formulate calibrated, growth-oriented performance feedback utilizing the Situation-Behavior-Impact (SBI) framework, balancing objective appraisal with actionable developmental coaching.

Context & Evaluation Profile:
- Reviewer Role: [e.g., Cross-Functional Product Manager / Peer Tech Lead / Direct Manager]
- Subject Role & Level: [e.g., Mid-Level Data Analyst]
- Demonstrated Strengths: [e.g., High-fidelity SQL query design, creative data pipeline architectures, positive attitude]
- Developmental Opportunity Areas: [e.g., Delays in sprint ticket delivery, failure to proactively communicate blockers in Slack, silent scope drift]
- Specific Incident: [e.g., Q3 Marketing Attribution Dashboard delayed by 10 days due to uncommunicated schema refactor]

Drafting Directives:
1. Apply the SBI model strictly: State the Situation, describe the observable Behavior without moralizing adjectives, and delineate the measurable operational Impact.
2. Avoid absolute generalizations (eliminate words like "always," "never," or "poor attitude") and ground criticism entirely in observable actions and business effects.
3. Formulate two concrete forward-looking behavioral agreements (e.g., "Post blocker notification in #project channel when milestone slips by >24 hrs").

Output Architecture:
1. Formal Review Submission Text: Standard appraisal entry (under 200 words) balanced across Achievements and Target Development Areas.
2. 1-on-1 Discussion Script: Talking points and framing statements for the in-person feedback conversation to minimize defensive reactions.
